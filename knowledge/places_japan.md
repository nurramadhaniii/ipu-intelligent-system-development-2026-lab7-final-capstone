# Places in Japan (日本の観光地)

## Chuson-ji (中尊寺) — Hiraizumi, Iwate (岩手県平泉町)
- Category: temple / 寺, Tendai Buddhism / 天台宗, UNESCO World Heritage / 世界遺産
- Highlights: Konjikido golden hall (金色堂); Tsukimizaka slope (月見坂) lined with old cedar trees
- Access: JR Hiraizumi Station (平泉駅), then Ichinoseki Hiraizumi Line or Iwate Kotsu bus to the "Chuson-ji temple" stop
- Cost: admission about 1,000 yen for adults (estimate)
- Best season: autumn leaves (紅葉)
- Source: https://hiraizumi.or.jp/en/archive/sightseeing/chusonji.html (checked 2026-11)

## Geibikei Gorge (猊鼻渓) — Ichinoseki, Iwate (岩手県一関市)
- Category: gorge / 渓谷, river boat ride / 舟下り, nature / 自然
- Highlights: flat-bottomed boat pushed by a boatman with a single pole along cliffs 50 to 100 m high; the boatman sings "Geibi Oiwake" (げいび追分); kotatsu boats (こたつ舟)
- Access: JR Ichinoseki Station, Ofunato Line (大船渡線) to Geibikei Station (猊鼻渓駅), then about 5 minutes on foot to the boat pier
- Cost: boat ride about 2,000 yen for adults, round trip about 90 minutes (estimate)
- Best season: fresh green (新緑) in spring, autumn leaves (紅葉), snow view (雪景色) in winter
- Source: https://www.gltjp.com/ja/directory/item/13759/ (checked 2026-11)

## Nanshoso (南昌荘) — Morioka, Iwate (岩手県盛岡市)
- Category: Japanese garden / 日本庭園, historic house / 邸宅, Meiji era / 明治
- Highlights: pond-circuit garden (池泉回遊式庭園) registered as a national monument; wooden house built around 1885 by businessman Segawa Yasugoro (瀬川安五郎); red maple leaves around the pond in autumn
- Access: from JR Morioka Station (盛岡駅) take the bus to "Shimonohashi-cho" (下の橋町) stop and walk about 5 minutes, or walk about 20 minutes from Morioka Station
- Cost: admission about 400 yen for adults (estimate)
- Best season: autumn leaves (紅葉)
- Source: https://www.gltjp.com/ja/directory/item/15787/ (checked 2026-11)

## Lake Towada (十和田湖) — Towada, Aomori (青森県十和田市)
- Category: lake / 湖, caldera lake / カルデラ湖, nature / 自然, national park / 国立公園
- Highlights: deep blue double caldera lake on the Aomori and Akita border; sightseeing boat (遊覧船) of about 50 minutes from Yasumiya (休屋); Otome no Zo statue (乙女の像); near Oirase Stream (奥入瀬渓流)
- Access: from JR Hachinohe Station (八戸駅) then take the JR bus Oirase bound for Lake Towada, about 2 hours 15 minutes; the boats run only from late April to early November
- Cost: walking around the lake is free; sightseeing boat about 1,650 yen for adults (estimate)
- Best season: autumn leaves (紅葉) from mid-October to early November; fresh green (新緑) in spring
- Source: https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80002225 (checked 2026-11)

## Universal Studios Japan (ユニバーサル・スタジオ・ジャパン) — Osaka, Osaka (大阪府大阪市)
- Category: theme park / テーマパーク, amusement / 遊園地, USJ
- Highlights: Super Nintendo World (スーパー・ニンテンドー・ワールド), The Wizarding World of Harry Potter (ハリー・ポッター), Minion Park (ミニオン), parades and seasonal shows
- Access: JR Yumesaki Line (ゆめ咲線) to Universal City Station (ユニバーサルシティ駅), then about 5 minutes on foot
- Cost: 1-Day Studio Pass from about 8,400 yen for adults (estimate)
- Best season: all year; Halloween (ハロウィーン) in autumn and Christmas (クリスマス) in winter; weekdays are less crowded
- Source: https://www.usj.co.jp/web/ja/jp/tickets/lineup (checked 2026-11)

## Senso-ji (浅草寺) — Asakusa, Tokyo (東京都台東区)
- Category: temple / 寺, Buddhism / 仏教, oldest temple in Tokyo / 都内最古の寺
- Highlights: Kaminarimon gate (雷門) with its giant red lantern (提灯); Nakamise shopping street (仲見世) with rice crackers and snacks; five-storied pagoda (五重塔)
- Access: Asakusa Station (浅草駅) on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Toei Asakusa Line or Tobu Line, about 5 minutes on foot
- Cost: free admission / 無料
- Best season: all year; the grounds are quiet early in the morning; hozuki market (ほおずき市) in July
- Source: https://www.gltjp.com/ja/article/item/10068/ (checked 2026-11)

## Art Aquarium Museum GINZA (アートアクアリウム美術館 GINZA) — Ginza, Tokyo (東京都中央区)
- Category: art museum / 美術館, aquarium / 水族館, goldfish / 金魚, indoor / 屋内
- Highlights: goldfish (金魚) in glass tanks combined with light, sound and scent; "Goldfish Corridor" (金魚の回廊) of round pillar tanks; seasonal exhibits with flowers (花)
- Access: Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Marunouchi Line, Hibiya Line Ginza Station Exit A7, right there. A 5-minute walk from Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line Ginza Isschome Station (Exit 9). A 2-minute walk from Toei Asakusa Line / Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line Higashi-Ginza Station via the underground passage toward Ginza Station. 9-minute walk from JR Yurakucho Station (Central Exit, Ginza Exit)
- Cost: about 2,700 yen for adults at the ticket machine, about 2,500 yen online; children of elementary age or younger free with an adult (estimate)
- Best season: all year, good on rainy or hot days; open 10:00 to 19:00, last entry 18:00
- Source: https://www.walkerplus.com/spot/ar0313s405201/map.html (checked 2026-11)

## Kamikochi (上高地) — Matsumoto, Nagano (長野県松本市)
- Category: mountain resort / 山岳リゾート, nature / 自然, hiking / ハイキング, national park / 国立公園
- Highlights: Kappa Bridge (河童橋) with a view of the Hotaka mountains (穂高連峰); clear Azusa River (梓川); Taisho Pond (大正池); about 1,500 m above sea level
- Access: Alpico train from JR Matsumoto Station (松本駅) to Shin-Shimashima Station (新島々駅), then bus to Kamikochi Bus Terminal, about 65 minutes; private cars are banned all year, so drivers park at Sawando (沢渡) and take a shuttle bus
- Cost: entering the area is free
- Best season: fresh green (新緑) from late April after the opening festival (開山祭); autumn leaves (紅葉) in October
- Source: https://www.nap-camp.com/mag/107634 (checked 2026-11)

## Nara Park (奈良公園) — Nara, Nara (奈良県奈良市)
- Category: park / 公園, deer / 鹿, history / 歴史, UNESCO World Heritage area / 世界遺産
- Highlights: about 1,300 wild deer (鹿), a national natural monument, that you can feed with deer crackers (鹿せんべい); Todai-ji Great Buddha (東大寺 大仏), Kasuga Taisha (春日大社) and Kofuku-ji (興福寺) nearby; Ukimido pavilion (浮見堂) on Sagi Pond
- Access: Kintetsu Nara Station (近鉄奈良駅), about 5 to 10 minutes on foot; from JR Nara Station (JR奈良駅) about 20 minutes on foot or a short city bus ride
- Cost: the park is free / 無料
- Best season: fawns (子鹿) shown in June; autumn leaves (紅葉); spring cherry blossoms (桜)
- Source: https://www.gltjp.com/ja/directory/item/11611/ (checked 2026-11)

## Mount Fuji (富士山) — Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi (山梨県富士吉田市)
- Category: mountain / 山, climbing / 登山, highest mountain in Japan / 日本最高峰, UNESCO World Cultural Heritage / 世界文化遺産
- Highlights: 3,776 m volcano; sunrise from the summit (ご来光) above a sea of clouds (雲海); the 5th Station (五合目) has views and food and is open to sightseers without the climbing rules
- Access: bus to the Fuji Subaru Line 5th Station (富士スバルライン五合目) on the Yoshida Trail (吉田ルート), including a direct bus from Shinjuku (新宿); climbers must book in advance, and the 5th Station gate is closed to climbers from 14:00 to 3:00
- Cost: Yoshida Trail climbing fee 4,000 yen per person; the trails in Shizuoka (静岡県) also charge 4,000 yen; visiting the 5th Station only is free (estimate)
- Best season: climbing season from July 1 to September 10 on the Yoshida Trail (the start can be delayed by weather or snow)
- Source: https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/fujisan/annzenn/documents/r8fujitozan.html (checked 2026-11)
